# Changelog · Reports card (native)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-28

### Added

- First version (#508): week, month, year and heating-season reports per
  mode (all, heating, hot water, cooling), read from the reports the
  integration stores for good.
- Drop-downs for the period type, the period (with ‹ › to step), the mode
  and the comparison (previous period, same period last year, none).
- Tabs: Overview (tiles with the change and a verdict per metric), Energy &
  COP (per mode, last 12 periods, per day, days left out of the COP),
  Temperatures (outdoor, leaving / return water, ΔT, flow, degree days),
  Cycling, Events & ML (backup heater, defrosts, alerts per type, ML
  anomalies) and Compare (side-by-side table and a chart of kWh per degree
  day and COP).
- English, Dutch and French; light and dark; visual editor; several heat
  pumps (choose one); Diagnostics for a bug report.
